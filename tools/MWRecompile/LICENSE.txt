MWRecompile - licence
=====================

MWRecompile is a command-line build of the MWEdit script compiler. It is
distributed under the MIT License, and may be freely used, copied, modified and
redistributed within the terms below.

This file has two parts:

  1. MWRecompile's own code and its changes to MWEdit (MIT, dej (dejunai)).
  2. The MWEdit code it is based on, which stays under its original MIT licence
     (Walrus Tech). That notice is kept below unchanged. Anyone who
     redistributes MWRecompile, or any part of it, must keep both notices.

MWEdit was originally written by Dave Humphrey (UESP). This build is based on
MWEdit 0.7.0 by Walrus Tech (https://github.com/Walrus-Tech/MWEdit, tag 0.7.0,
commit 6dfaf72). Every change made for MWRecompile is marked "MWRecompile" in
the source and listed in CHANGES.md.

===============================================================================
1. MWRecompile modifications and additions
   This covers:
     - cli/ (the command-line front end, the headless stubs and the tools);
     - the Makefile and val/;
     - every change marked "MWRecompile" in vendor/ (see CHANGES.md and
       CHANGES-vendor.diff).
